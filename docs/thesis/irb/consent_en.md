## What is this study about?
We are testing software that tells real people apart from automated programs
("bots") on websites, using how a page is used rather than puzzles like CAPTCHAs.

## What will I do?
For about 10–15 minutes you will use a test shopping website: log in with a
**dummy account we give you**, search, look at products and do a fake checkout.
Please do **not** enter any real personal data or passwords.

## What is recorded?
While you use the site, your browser calculates summary numbers such as average
mouse speed, how long keys are held down, and how fast you scroll. Only these
numbers are stored, with a study code instead of your name.

**We do not record** which keys you press or what you type, screenshots or video,
your camera, microphone or location, or anything outside the study website. Your
IP address and browser details are used only during the visit and are not stored.

## Risks and benefits
The risk is minimal. There is no direct benefit to you; you help research on
protecting websites from abuse.

## Confidentiality
Your name appears only on this form, which is kept separately from the data.
Results are reported only as group statistics. Data are stored securely and
deleted after **[retention period]**.

## Voluntary participation

with the university. You may stop at any time, and you may ask to have your
data deleted within **[2 weeks]** by quoting your study code.

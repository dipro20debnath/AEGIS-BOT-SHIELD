## What is this study about?
We are testing software that tells real people apart from automated programs
("bots") on websites, using how a page is used rather than puzzles like CAPTCHAs.

## What will I do?
For about 10–15 minutes you will use a test shopping website. You will answer
a few optional questions, then do six small tasks:
- log in with a **dummy account shown on the screen**;
- search for and compare products;
- change your cart;
- copy invented delivery details into a form;
- write one or two sentences of a review.

Please do **not** enter any real personal data or passwords. You can skip any
task or question.

## What is recorded?
While you use the site, your browser calculates summary numbers such as average
mouse speed, how long keys are held down, and how fast you scroll. Also stored:
- these numbers, for each page;
- your answers to the optional questions;
- how long each task took and whether it was completed;
- your browser and operating system type (e.g. "Chrome on Android").

Everything is stored under a study code instead of your name.

**Optional:** you can also allow us to record the timing of your individual
mouse movements, key presses and scrolling: when each happened and where on
the screen. For keys we record only the kind of key (letter, space,
Backspace…), never which key or what you typed. This helps the research a
lot, but you can take part without it.

**We do not record** which keys you press or what you type, screenshots or video,
your camera, microphone or location, or anything outside the study website. Your
IP address and the full details of your browser are used only during the visit
and are not stored.

## Risks and benefits
The risk is minimal. There is no direct benefit to you; you help research on
protecting websites from abuse.

## Confidentiality
Your name is kept only on the researcher's separate list (and this form, if you
sign it on paper), never together with the data. Results are reported only as
group statistics. Data are stored securely and deleted after **[retention period]**.

## Voluntary participation

with the university. You may stop at any time by closing the website, and you
may ask to have your data deleted within **[2 weeks]** by quoting your study code.
